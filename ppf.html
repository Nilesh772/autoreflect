Invisible Self-Healing Polyurethane Shield

Paint Protection Film (PPF)

Ensure the ultimate preservation of your vehicle. Paint Protection Film (also known as Clear Bra) is an optically clear, high-impact thermoplastic urethane film applied directly to your paint. It acts as an impact-absorbing shield that completely eliminates rock chip damage, sandblasting, and key scratches.

Heat-Activated Self-Healing Technology

Our premium PPF features an advanced elastomeric top coat that possesses self-healing memory. When exposed to heat from the sun, warm engine bay heat, or hot water, the microscopic polymers rearrange themselves to seal away micro-swirls, wash scratches, and rub marks automatically.

We believe detailing is not just cleaning—it is a restoration process. Every angle of paint is checked under spectrum light rigs before we continue, ensuring absolute perfection across every contour.

Computer Cut & Wrapped Invisible Fit

Using our database of digital vehicle patterns, we cut specific film panels matched to your exact vehicle model, trim, and sensors. Our expert installers wrap the film around all panel edges inside door jams and headlights, making the film installation virtually invisible to the naked eye.

Our dedication to quality means we allocate extended times to each car. We never rush our curings, compounds, or wraps. Your vehicle deserves dedicated hours of manual craftsmanship.
