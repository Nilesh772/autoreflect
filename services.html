Our Detailing Treatments

Preserving, Correcting, and Shielding Your Vehicle's Surfaces

Ceramic Coating

Nanotechnology liquid glass that bonds at a molecular level with your vehicle's factory paint, forming a semi-permanent sacrificial barrier.

  • Warranty: 3 to 7 Years
  • Time Required: 2 - 3 Days
  • Hydrophobic water-beading effect
  • Shields from UV, bird droppings, & salt
  • Unmatched deep liquid gloss

PPF (Paint Protection Film)

The ultimate armor. An optically clear thermoplastic urethane film applied to high-impact areas or the full vehicle to shield against physical damage.

  • Warranty: up to 10 Years
  • Time Required: 3 - 5 Days
  • Self-heals swirl marks using heat
  • Complete rock chip & key scratch defense
  • Precision edge-wrapped invisible install

Paint Correction

A rigorous leveling process that removes paint defects, restoring clarity. We refine your clear coat to reveal the paint's true color and metallic flake depth.

  • Result: Removes 85-95% defects
  • Time Required: 1 - 2 Days
  • Eradicates swirls, oxidation, and scratches
  • Restores flat paint reflections
  • Preparation for coating packages

Premium Car Wash

Not a simple wash, but a 21-step luxury decontamination. Safe washing techniques that thoroughly clean your vehicle without introducing paint swirls.

  • Duration: 2 to 4 Hours
  • Process: Multi-bucket hand wash

  • Deep wheel-barrel cleaning & iron de-con
  • Interior vacuum & dashboard dressing
