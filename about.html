About AUTOREFLECT

The Mirror Finish Experts

Crafting Premium Car Aesthetics

AUTOREFLECT was born out of a pure, uncompromising passion for automotive beauty. What started as a boutique garage has grown into the city’s premier auto detailing studio, catering to enthusiasts, luxury collectors, and daily drivers who demand nothing short of perfection.

We believe that every car has a hidden depth. Over years of road wear, weathering, and improper washing, paint develops micro-scratches and oxidation that act like a veil, hiding its true color. Our mission is to lift that veil, restoring the deep, glossy, liquid-like mirror finish underneath.

Our studio is equipped with advanced lighting rigs, temperature-stabilized curing bays, dust-extraction filtration, and the absolute finest detailing products sourced globally to guarantee world-class results.

Our Core Detailing Standards

The uncompromising details that set our studio apart from regular detailing franchises.

Uncompromising Craftsmanship

When you leave your vehicle with us, you are leaving it in a controlled, premium environment where every detail is monitored.

  • Full Digital Paint Analysis: We measure paint thickness using ultrasonic depth gauges before any machine polishing to ensure your clear coat remains safe and thick.
  • Dust & Climate-Controlled Studio: Our coating applications are carried out in a cleanroom-style environment, ensuring zero dust contamination under the ceramic bonding.
  • Ultra-Premium Product Lineup: We work exclusively with world-renowned surface coatings and self-healing films including Gtechniq, CarPro, Kamikaze, and XPEL.
  • Infrared Coating Curing: We cure our ceramic coatings using short-wave infrared lamps, speeding up bonding times and locking in a deeper finish before hand-off.
